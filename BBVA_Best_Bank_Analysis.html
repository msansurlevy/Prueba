The other side: risk concentration

• Mexico is a major contributor to BBVA’s earnings
• Emerging-market exposure can increase currency and macroeconomic volatility
• Credit risk differs substantially across operating markets
• Higher returns may partly compensate investors for higher risk

The key issue is not just how much BBVA earns, but the risk it takes to earn it.

How does BBVA compare globally?

Final assessment

BBVA has a compelling case as one of the world’s best-performing banks, particularly in profitability, efficiency and customer experience.

But “the best” is too strong without a comparable global, risk-adjusted assessment.

The better question: best for whom, and measured by what?
